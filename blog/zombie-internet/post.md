# Zombie Internet

Over the past few years, the open web has shifted rapidly from human-curated discussions and blogs to synthetic text generation at scale. In this deep dive, we examine what dead internet theories got right, what they got wrong, and how algorithmic synthesis is rewriting the nature of online discovery.
